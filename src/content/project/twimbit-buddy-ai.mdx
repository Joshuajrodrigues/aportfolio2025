# Overview

Twimbit is a tech-forward research and advisory firm providing market insights and contextual intelligence to business leaders.

As the sole product designer, I worked directly with the founders to turn an initial internal initiative into an insight-driven platform.

# The Hiccup

Content analysts faced continuous friction between drafting research and publishing inside the proprietary CMS.

The team also experienced fragmented adoption across external AI tools, lacking a central workspace that integrated proprietary firm data.

# The Idea

Buddy combines access to models like Claude, ChatGPT, and Perplexity into a single operational interface.

The platform pairs these base engines with custom assistants and Twimbit's private intelligence repository to support daily analytical workflows.

# The Process

I conducted discovery interviews with analysts across the research division to trace routine editorial bottlenecks.

Working alongside frontend engineers and the AI systems team, I mapped core requirements, defined assistive workflows, and built out interface prototypes.

# The Impact

The internal rollout drove a 30% lift in publishing productivity and generated strong stakeholder demand to package the system as an external product.

Client demonstrations led to a 20% rise in new platform inquiries.
