# Overview

ActOut is an independent print magazine concept highlighting non-mainstream musical talent through in-depth profiles and reviews.

The project explores physical editorial design as an alternative to algorithmic streaming feeds, giving listeners space for deliberate engagement.

# The Hiccup

Algorithmic discovery creates passive listening habits dominated by viral trends, making it difficult for independent artists to sustain focused attention.

Translating contemporary music commentary into print requires pacing that avoids visual clutter while establishing an identity distinct from legacy rock publications.

# The Idea

A monthly publication built on generous negative space, intentional rhythm, and a recognizable chromatic palette.

The visual direction leverages a signature green to anchor familiarity with modern audio platforms, while the interior spreads prioritize breathing room, photographic storytelling, and artist intent.

# The Process

I established typographic hierarchies and multi-column grid layouts designed to balance extended editorial prose with album artwork.

I developed spread pacing around resting points, curated artist profiles, and produced full high-fidelity layout proofs across feature stories and review sections.

# The Impact

The concept established a fully realized publication blueprint spanning cover treatments, grid architectures, and feature spreads.

The design system demonstrates how physical editorial media can foster deeper cultural connection and curated discovery outside digital ecosystems.
