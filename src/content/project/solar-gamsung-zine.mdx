# Overview

This publication stems from personal engagement with Solar's self-titled vocal project covering emotive, historical Korean songs.

Because physical editions of these releases are scarce in India, I designed an independent, tangible piece of archival memorabilia to document and commemorate the music.

# The Hiccup

Translating an auditory and cinematic experience into print requires capturing specific tonal nuances without relying on high-gloss commercial aesthetics.

The visual direction needed to honor the video's vast, solitary atmosphere while seamlessly presenting both original Hangul lyricism and personal reflections.

# The Idea

A minimalist editorial zine rendered entirely in black and white to evoke isolation, snowy landscapes, and quiet contemplation.

The design relies on matte, textured stock rather than glossy finishes, pairing Korean typography with reflective English prose to establish a restrained, tactile archive.

# The Process

I deconstructed the music video, cinematography, and archival production footage to extract recurring visual themes, contrast ratios, and spatial balance.

I set the compositions around the original Korean lyrics, curated type pairings suited for bilingual reading, and specified uncoated paper stocks to emphasize print grain and tactile presence.

# The Impact

The project culminated in a finished, production-ready editorial layout that translates digital audiovisual work into an enduring personal publication.

The publication served as an exploration into bilingual typography, material print choices, and independent physical archiving.
