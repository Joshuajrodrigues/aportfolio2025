# Overview

Twimbit is a research and advisory firm delivering market intelligence through contextual, AI-driven platforms.

As the sole product designer over a three-year period, I steered the web experience through multiple operational and visual evolutions alongside leadership.

# The Hiccup

The original web presence operated on an inherited, rigid system that failed to communicate the company's shift toward contextual AI products.

Rapid startup pivots created inconsistencies across navigation, conversion funnels, and brand messaging.

# The Idea

Rebuild the core architecture to function as a modular, insight-led web platform with distinct microsites.

The layout structure focused on clear service articulation, improved reading engagement for research reports, and simplified conversion paths.

# The Process

I partnered with an external agency to overhaul the underlying design system and visual language to match stakeholder objectives.

From that system, I designed dozens of high-intent pages, led the internal brand rollout, and structured microsites to support new business verticals.

# The Impact

Two successive architectural overhauls drove a 200% increase in platform engagement events.

The platform stabilized into a self-sustaining web ecosystem supporting recurring launches, with higher inbound qualified inquiries from enterprise clients.
